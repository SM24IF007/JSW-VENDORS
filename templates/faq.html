QUESTIONS

Before you apply.

Straight answers about the public application, joining process, contractor assignment and statutory information.

The public application begins with essential details such as name, mobile number, qualification and selected role. Detailed identity, address and statutory information is handled later in the authorized admin workflow.
The portal does not publish a fixed shift duration because it depends on the assigned role, department and contractor requirement. The confirmed schedule is communicated during processing or joining.
No fixed public verification time is promised. Timing depends on document readiness, role review and contractor processing. Use the application-status page for the latest recorded stage.
Applicants do not self-select a contractor from the partner section. Contractor mapping follows the selected role, review and admin joining process.
Open the Check Application Status page and enter the same 10-digit mobile number used during registration.
After application, verification and interview details are completed, the three-page joining record is prepared for review. Final plant access remains subject to the applicable contractor and plant approvals.
The portal supports recording PF/UAN and ESIC information in the joining workflow. Actual statutory coverage depends on eligibility, applicable rules and the employment record.
