JSW STEEL · VASIND WORKS

Steel finishing at scale.
Built for consistency.

Vasind Works is a downstream steel-processing facility in Maharashtra focused on cold rolled, galvanised and other value-added products for automotive, appliance and industrial applications.

ABOUT VASIND WORKS

Downstream steel.
Digitally controlled.

Public JSW Steel material describes Vasind as a value-addition hub that converts hot rolled steel into cold rolled and coated products. Automation, real-time process monitoring and digital quality checks support consistent surface finish, coating thickness and dimensional accuracy.

01

Cold rolling & coating

Advanced processing lines convert incoming steel into finished cold rolled and coated products for demanding end uses.

02

Real-time quality control

Digital monitoring tracks surface quality, coating thickness and dimensional accuracy across production batches.

03

Resource efficiency

JSW highlights closed-loop water reuse, energy-efficient equipment, waste recovery and emissions-control systems at the facility.

04

Value-added product mix

JSW's annual reporting lists Vasind among its downstream operations for GI/GL, colour-coated products and CRCA.

PUBLIC MILESTONES

Public milestones that show Vasind's evolution.

The public facility profile highlights two important 2013 updates — cleaner-fuel transition and CCL-1 commissioning — before the story continues into today's downstream value-added steel processing.

2013Fuel transition

JSW's facility page records a switch from LPG to RLNG, indicating an operational move toward cleaner and more reliable fuel use.

2013CCL-1 commissioned

The same public profile lists commissioning of CCL-1, marking a visible strengthening of coated-steel processing capability at the site.

TodayDownstream value addition

Today, Vasind continues to support cold rolled and coated steel applications across multiple industrial segments through its downstream value-addition role.

QUESTIONS

Before you apply.

Practical answers about documents, shifts, verification, contractor assignment, application status and gate-pass processing.

Aadhaar Card, PAN Card and bank account proof are the compulsory base documents. Educational or technical documents are required according to the selected role and skill category.

Shift duration depends on the assigned role, department and contractor requirement. The confirmed schedule is communicated during processing or joining rather than being assumed on the website.

A typical joining process takes about 3–8 days when documents are ready. Punch/gate-card processing can take around 10–15 days and may vary with plant or contractor approvals.

Applicants do not self-select a firm from the partner section. Contractor mapping follows the role, review and admin joining process.

Use the Check Application Status section and enter the 10-digit mobile number used during registration.

After documents, review and interview/assessment are completed, the three-page joining record is prepared. Contractor and plant approvals then continue, and punch/gate-card processing may take around 10–15 days.
